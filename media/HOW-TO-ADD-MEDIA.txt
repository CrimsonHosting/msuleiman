NOTES
-----
- MP4 is much smaller than GIF and loops just as well. If a GIF is over ~10 MB,
  convert it: it will load faster and look sharper.
- Videos autoplay muted and loop, exactly like a GIF would.
- Any slot with no file shows a labelled "awaiting media" placeholder instead of
  breaking, so you can add files one at a time.
- Landscape 16:10 looks best in the large frames. The six small behaviour cards
  crop to 2:1, so keep the drone roughly centred in those clips.
